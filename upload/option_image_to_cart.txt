Ocextensions
------------
option image to cart

Installation
~~~~~~~~~~~~

        Make sure the FTP form in admin>site>settings is completed.
        Download extension - do not unzip or change name or do anything
        Go to "Extension Installer" in admin and upload the zipped file.
        Go to "Modifications" check the extension is listed and enabled then click on the refresh button.

Description
~~~~~~~~~~~
For products with option types "Image" , "Radio", "Checkbox" and "Select" that have an image, the option image will be displayed in the main and mini shopping cart.

It is best if only one option for a product has images. This allows the one option to be the parent and the others children, in other words if you have a t-shirt in various colours the parent selection could be the colour (with images) and the sizes (without images) the child. In this case the selected colour image will be displayed in the cart.
